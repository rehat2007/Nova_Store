About Nova

Fewer, better things.

Nova started with a simple frustration: most "essentials" wear out after a season. We work with a small number of mills to source heavier cottons and cleaner constructions, then keep the designs quiet enough to wear for years, not weeks.

Every piece is designed in-house, tested on our own team first, and priced to reflect the materials — not a marketing budget.
